## Homepage Inspiration

Reference file:

`references/homepage-inspiration.png`

The homepage should take visual and structural inspiration from this editorial website mockup.

What I like about it:

- Editorial, magazine-like composition
- Oversized expressive headings
- Layered photography and typography
- Artful asymmetry
- Occasional image and text overlap
- Organic image framing
- A mixture of full-width and contained sections
- Different visual moments as the visitor scrolls
- Strong personality without feeling cluttered
- A site that feels intentionally designed rather than template-driven

Translate those qualities into the Morgan Storm brand.

The Morgan Storm homepage should remain clear and professional while feeling warm, artistic, memorable, and slightly unconventional.

The homepage must clearly direct visitors toward:

1. Web & Digital Strategy
2. Ayurvedic Wellness
3. Portfolio

Do not let the editorial styling interfere with navigation, readability, accessibility, or calls to action.
